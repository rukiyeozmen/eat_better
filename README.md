# 🥗 Eat Better

> A personal nutrition mentor app that helps users understand what they eat, track their nutrition, and make better food decisions.

**Eat Better** is a Flutter-based nutrition app currently under development. The goal is to combine food recognition, personalized nutrition calculations, meal tracking, and AI-powered guidance into one simple experience.

---

## 🚀 Project Vision

Eat Better is being designed as more than a calorie tracker.

The long-term goal is to create a **personal nutrition mentor** that can:

* 📸 Analyze a photo of a meal
* 🍽️ Identify foods and estimate nutrition
* 🔢 Calculate calories and macronutrients
* 📊 Calculate BMI, BMR, and TDEE
* 🎯 Create personalized calorie targets
* 📝 Track meals throughout the day
* 💡 Recommend what to eat next
* ⏰ Suggest appropriate meal timing
* 🤖 Provide AI-powered nutrition guidance
* 🌍 Support multiple languages

## 🏗️ Planned Architecture

```text
Flutter App
     │
     ▼
FastAPI Backend
     │
     ├── AI Food Recognition
     │
     ├── Nutrition Database
     │
     └── User Data
```

The Flutter application will handle the user experience while the backend will manage AI processing, nutrition data, and user information.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of Eat Better is to make nutrition guidance **simple, practical, and personalized**.

Instead of only telling users how many calories they have eaten, the app aims to answer a more useful question:

> **"What should I eat next?"**
